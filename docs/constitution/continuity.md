# Continuity

Status: Draft, awaiting owner ratification

Launching the Guestbook means accepting responsibility for tending it. At the beginning, the custodian is the founder.

## Recurring duties

The custodian commits to at least:

- reviewing the moderation queue;
- answering privacy and deletion requests within legal deadlines;
- dealing with spam and abuse;
- checking that submissions still work;
- maintaining authentication, security updates, dependencies and hosting;
- maintaining backups and testing restores;
- checking that archive exports remain valid and recoverable;
- reviewing external references for disappearance or material change;
- maintaining the French and English interface text and handling problematic translations;
- updating contextual material;
- producing the annual edition and documenting corrections and withdrawals;
- keeping the domain registered and project documentation current.

These duties are listed with their frequency in an operations calendar (`docs/operations/`, #33).

## Design for one person

1. Every feature is judged partly by the permanent work it creates. Features that add a lot of operational burden without serving the archive are excluded.
2. Automation is encouraged where it reduces repetitive work, but automation does not remove custody. Automated jobs report failures to the custodian.
3. If the custodian cannot keep up, the project slows down openly (for example by pausing submissions) rather than decaying silently.

## Beyond the founder

The project is documented so that another technically competent person could understand what the archive is, where its data lives, how editions are produced, how deletion works, what services it depends on, what recurring duties exist and how to restore it from backups. Knowledge does not live only in the founder's memory or in chat transcripts.

The MVP does not require an organization. It must make a transition possible: to additional maintainers, an association, institutional archival partners, independent mirrors or a successor custodian. A succession arrangement covering the domain, hosting accounts, credentials and editions is prepared before public launch.

## If the project ends

If the project must close, the custodian announces it in advance, stops accepting submissions, publishes a final edition, deposits it with at least one durable institution where lawful, keeps permanent links resolving for as long as practical, and honours outstanding privacy requests.
