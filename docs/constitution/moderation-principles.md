# Moderation principles

Status: Draft, awaiting owner ratification

## Purpose

Entries are public publications, so nothing is published automatically. Every submission passes through light pre-publication moderation.

Moderation exists only to address:

- spam, automated flooding and repeated reposting;
- illegal material;
- threats and harassment;
- content that creates serious privacy problems for another person;
- accidental publication of secrets or credentials;
- obviously malicious use of the platform, including entries whose main purpose is advertising or SEO;
- violations of the published, narrow publishing rules.

## Limits

Moderation is not editorial or ideological gatekeeping. An entry is not rejected because the moderator disagrees with it, finds it poorly argued, considers its prediction foolish or believes its view of AI is mistaken. The archive is valuable partly because people disagree and are sometimes wrong.

## Practice

1. The publishing rules are public, short and written in plain language in French and English. Moderation decisions cite them.
2. Prefer the least intrusive action: redact a third party's name or a leaked credential rather than reject the entry, and ask the contributor when practical.
3. The original submission and any moderated public representation are stored separately. Redactions are recorded with their reason. The public page indicates that a redaction occurred, without revealing what was removed.
4. Every decision records who decided, when and why, in a private moderation record.
5. The contributor is told the outcome and, for rejections, the rule applied.
6. Moderation applies the same rules regardless of the contributor's views or identity, including when an entry criticizes the project or its operator.
7. The moderator's attention is a resource to protect. Tooling and anti-abuse measures should make spam cheap to clear so that real entries get careful review.

## Who moderates

For the MVP, the founder moderates alone. Additional moderators may be added later; they would follow these principles and their decisions would be recorded in the same way.
