# 0004. The operator is publicly named; the mentions légales use the non-professional route

Status: Accepted (2026-11-15)

Issue: #19

## Context

An archive asks contributors and future readers to trust its custody and provenance, which favours a named custodian.

LCEN art. 1-1 I 1 requires a natural-person publisher to publish their name, domicile and telephone number. Art. 1-1 II lets a non-professional publisher publish only the host's name and address, provided they have given their identification details to the host. The owner recalled that no physical address is needed. A follow-up search confirmed this for non-professional publishers ([legal research, section 1, follow-up](../research/legal-landscape.md#follow-up-2026-11-15-no-domicile-under-the-art-1-1-ii-route)).

## Decision

1. Raphaël Bardini is publicly named as the custodian and operator on the About page.
2. The mentions légales use the art. 1-1 II route. They state that the site is published on a non-professional basis by a private individual who has given their identification details to the host, followed by the host's name, address and telephone number. No domicile and no personal phone number are published.
3. A project contact address (not a personal mailbox) is published for contributors, privacy requests and legal notices.

## Consequences

- Provenance and accountability are clear, without publishing a home address.
- The art. 1-1 II route depends on the project staying non-professional: no advertising, no affiliate links, no paid services. Any change there, including accepting donations (not researched), reopens this ADR.
- The identification details must actually be lodged with the VPS host (#9, #26). This is a launch checklist item.
- Residual uncertainty, recorded as inference: no source discusses voluntarily naming oneself while using the art. 1-1 II route. The text grants a permission to disclose less, not a prohibition on disclosing more. Revisit if an authority says otherwise.
- The operator's name is attached to every moderation outcome. Moderation principles must be public and applied consistently ([constitution](../constitution/moderation-principles.md)).
